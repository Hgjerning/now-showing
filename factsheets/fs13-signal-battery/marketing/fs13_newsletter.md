**Every signal, every corner (FS13).** I ran all 19 price signals in six markets and set them against the 153 published JKP factors to find what works everywhere. After correcting for 38 tests no price signal earns a positive return that survives on its own; thirteen years of large caps can't prove any one signal. The direction is more consistent: momentum is positive in 5 of 6 markets and every published segment, low beta in 6 of 6 once the market bet is removed, borrow is paid and leverage financed, and debt issuance and profit growth lead the fundamentals. The 19 signals are really two bets, low risk and momentum. Seasonality and skewness lose in large caps, and Scandinavia disagrees with everyone.
