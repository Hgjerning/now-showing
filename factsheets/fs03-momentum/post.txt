Stocks that did best over the past year keep outperforming the worst over the next months. Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) found about 1% a month for US stocks in 1965–1989; Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013) found it in every major equity market and asset class.

I tested it on six markets, 2013–2026: US, Europe, UK, Denmark, Scandinavia and the world. Point-in-time index members, after costs.

Long/short (winners minus losers): -2.8% to +9.7% a year, positive in 5 of six markets. Adjusted for market beta: +1.8% to +15.8%.
Long-only (winners): a better Sharpe than owning all the stocks equally in 5 of six markets.

The pre-declared fixes (beta-neutral legs, a turnover buffer, volatility targeting) took the 2020–26 holdout Sharpe from 0.29 to 0.55.
