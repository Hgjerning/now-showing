# Pre-registration: A15 "Viva Las Vegas": do lottery stocks still lose? (2026-09-25)

**Written before any number below was computed.**

## 1. Data (fixed)

- JKP US factor `rmax1_21d` (long low-MAX, short high-MAX, capped value-weighted terciles),
  monthly through Dec 2025. Bali, Cakici & Whitelaw (2011) used data to 2005 and published in 2011.
- Large caps: the A06 point-in-time top-500 universe (Sharadar), 2013-01 → 2026-08. MAX is the
  largest daily return in the formation month. Portfolios are equal-weighted deciles held one month.

## 2. Trials (two; one-sided gate p < 0.025)

| trial | hypothesis | window | statistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A15-1** | Low-MAX beats high-MAX after publication (broad US market, JKP) | Jan 2012 → Dec 2025 | mean > 0, NW t (6 lags) |
| **A15-2** | Low-MAX beats high-MAX in the 500 largest stocks | Feb 2013 → Aug 2026 | decile 1 minus decile 10, mean > 0, NW t (3 lags) |

**Reported:** the pre-publication JKP period; the 2020–21 retail/"meme" window; beta and
volatility of the extreme deciles.

## 3. Expected outcomes

- **A15-1: fail (about 40% pass).**
- **A15-2: fail (about 30%).** Lottery demand is a small-cap retail phenomenon.
