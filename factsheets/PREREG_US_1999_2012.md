# Pre-registration · US 1999–2012 on Sharadar: every US strategy, out of sample

Written 2 October 2026, **before any 1998–2012 signal or strategy return was computed.** The only things looked at so far are
coverage counts and data checks (section 3). Nothing is re-tuned: every signal, book, cost and gate is copied from the
2013–2026 work. The registered 2013–2026 verdicts stand whatever this shows; this study can only add a second period beside them.

## 1. Question

Do the US results of the factsheets (FS01–FS21) and of the live book's US leg (Project2, deployed config `f44cfc13dcec8ccb`)
hold in 1999–2012, a period none of them was built on, which contains the 2000–02 and 2008 bear markets?

Why this is new and not a repeat of FS15 or the Project2 ART test: those used the ART archive, with a universe of the 500
largest stocks by traded value and no index dates. This uses a second, independent vendor (Sharadar), the actual
point-in-time S&P 500 membership (the same universe definition as the 2013–2026 US cells, `FS_US_UNIVERSE=sp500_pit`), and
as-reported fundamentals dated by filing. Agreement with FS15 and the ART test is itself reported (section 6).

## 3. Checks already done (not trials; no returns of any strategy computed)

- Membership rebuilt from events matches Sharadar's own quarterly member lists 100% at every March snapshot 1998–2012.
- Prices exist for 100% of members in every year 1998–2012 (99.6% at the 1997 year-end); fundamentals for 96–100% from 1998.
- 2012 daily returns are identical to today's US cache for all 539 common tickers.
- 252 daily moves beyond ±50% out of 2.6 million; the ones inspected are real events (bankruptcies, the 2003 HealthSouth collapse).

## 4. Windows (fixed)

- **Factsheets:** first portfolio month 1999-01, last 2012-12 (168 months). Signals may use data from 1998-01 (no lookback
  exceeds 12 months). No overlap with the 2013-02 start of the registered factsheet window.
- **Project2 US leg:** weekly, first rebalance 1999-01-08, last 2011-12-30 (13 years). Stops before Project2's in-sample
  window (2012-01-13 onward).
- Sub-periods reported for every test: 1999–2005 and 2006–2012.

## 5. Tests and pass rules (fixed)

Classification for every replicated cell, as in FS15: **confirmed** = same sign as 2013–2026 and |t| > 2;
**consistent** = same sign, |t| ≤ 2; **reversed** = sign flips. Where the 2013–2026 |t| is below 1 there is no
in-sample effect to replicate; those cells are reported with their t and classified **no prior**.
A **new** claim of edge from 1999–2012 alone needs the factsheet gate |t| > 2.87 and is then still checked against the
programme-wide Bonferroni bar over the updated trial count.

## 6. Sensitivities fixed now (reported, none chosen)

1. **Delisting returns.** Sharadar's last price is the last trade; a final delisting loss may be missing, which flatters
   long legs that hold failing firms. Rerun with −30% on the last month of any ticker whose S&P 500 removal note names
   bankruptcy or a ticker ending in Q.
2. **Costs before decimalisation.** Rerun with costs doubled before April 2001 (US tick sizes were fractions until then).
3. **Universe lag.** Membership applied one month late.

## 7. Trial count and bars

65 new trials (22 + 19 + 20 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1). The factsheet ledger (682 at its last build) is rebuilt with them and the
programme-wide Bonferroni and Benjamini–Hochberg bars are recomputed and reported with the results. Project2's own ledger
gets one trial (T7).

## 8. What will not be done

- No signal, parameter, universe rule, cost or gate is changed after seeing any 1999–2012 return.
- A harness bug found during the run may be fixed **before** the verdict table is read; every such fix is logged here with
  its effect, as in the ART test.
- No cell is dropped or added. A test that cannot be run is reported as "not run" with the reason.
- Results are published in full, failures included; the factsheets get a 1999–2012 box, not a rewrite.
