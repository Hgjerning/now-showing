What we count, and what we don't

The games, the tree and the cinema page are free and need no sign-up. We want to know which parts readers use, so a few things are counted. Nothing identifies you.

Counted (anonymously)

  • Page visits, with the website you came from, through GoatCounter, an open-source counter that sets no cookies and keeps no personal data.
  • Clicks: a branch opened, a game started or finished, a score, each guess in the games, a vote or "ticket", and the optional "who are you?" choice (professional investor, private investor, student or academic, other). These are counts only, with no name or address attached.

Kept only in your own browser

  • Your best game score, the votes you have cast (so a button shows "Vote counted") and the date of your last visit (so new items can be marked NEW). This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.
  • A name you type in a game goes only into the challenge link you choose to share. We do not store or count it.

Not collected

  • No names, e-mail addresses, IP addresses kept by us, cookies, advertising or tracking across sites. No external fonts.

Requests

A request to test your own strategy is a message you send yourself on LinkedIn; nothing is sent from this site.
